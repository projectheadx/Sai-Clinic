Our Inspiring Journey

From a Small Room to 6 Clinics

12 years ago, in a small room in Kavaraipettai, Dr. Samraj and Dr. Sangami started their dream - a small clinic with a big vision. With limited resources but unlimited determination, they began serving the community with compassionate healthcare. Today, SAI CLINIC has grown into a network of 6 successful clinics across different locations, providing quality healthcare to thousands of patients.

"We started with a single room, a stethoscope, and a dream. Today, we have 6 clinics across different locations. Every patient's smile is our biggest achievement."

— Dr. Samraj & Dr. Sangami
