Free Eye Screening Camp at SAI CLINIC - Kannikaiper

August 30, 2024 Dr. S. Sangami Kannikaiper, Thiruvallur Dist

Kannikaiper, August 30: A free eye screening camp was successfully conducted today at SAI CLINIC, Kannikaiper, organized by SAI CLINIC and Wilg Eye Clinic.

The camp was held at No. 6, Karunya School Opposite, Kannikaiper and was led by Dr. S. Sangami and Dr. T.S. Vinsy Marjolin.

Over 100 people benefited from the camp!

The camp provided free eye check-ups including:

  • 👁️ Eye screening and vision testing
  • 🔬 Glaucoma screening
  • 🩻 Retinal examination for diabetic patients
  • 💉 Blood pressure check-up

Additionally, spectacles were provided at subsidized rates for those in need. Free eye surgeries were arranged for patients requiring surgical intervention.

The camp was well-received by the community, with participants appreciating the quality of care and the free services provided. SAI CLINIC continues its commitment to serving the community with accessible and affordable healthcare.

Diabetes Awareness Month - Know Your Risk

September 2024 Dr. S. Sangami Health Tips

September is Diabetes Awareness Month, and SAI CLINIC is dedicated to helping you understand your risk and take control of your health.

Did you know? Over 70 million people in India have diabetes.

Early Signs of Diabetes

  • 🚽 Frequent urination, especially at night
  • 💧 Increased thirst and hunger
  • ⚖️ Unexplained weight loss
  • 🫨 Fatigue and weakness
  • 👁️ Blurred vision

Risk Factors

  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family history of diabetes
  • ⚖️ Obesity or overweight
  • 🚶 Sedentary lifestyle
  • 🍬 Unhealthy diet
  • 📈 High blood pressure

SAI CLINIC offers comprehensive diabetes screening and management programs. Early detection can prevent complications.
