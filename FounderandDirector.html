Our Inspiring Journey

Dr. Samraj & Dr. Sangami

12 years ago, in a small room in Kavaraipettai, Dr. Samraj and Dr. Sangami started their dream - a small clinic with a big vision. With limited resources but unlimited determination, they began serving the community with compassionate healthcare.

"We started with a single room, a stethoscope, and a dream. Today, we have 6 clinics across different locations. Every patient's smile is our biggest achievement."

— Dr. Samraj & Dr. Sangami

Today, SAI CLINIC has grown into a network of 6 successful clinics operating in different locations, providing quality healthcare to thousands of patients. From a small room to a healthcare empire - this is the story of passion, perseverance, and purpose.

Our Journey Timeline

The story of SAI CLINIC - from one room to six locations

2012

🏥 The Beginning

Dr. Samraj and Dr. Sangami started SAI CLINIC in a small room at Kavaraipettai with just basic medical equipment and unlimited passion for healthcare.

2015

📈 First Expansion

Moved to a larger facility in Kavaraipettai with improved infrastructure. Added Lab services and basic diagnostic equipment.

2018

🏢 Second Clinic

Opened the second SAI CLINIC branch in a nearby location. Started offering X-Ray and ECG services.

2021

🏥 Network Expansion

Expanded to 4 clinics with full-fledged diagnostic facilities including digital X-Ray, ECG, and full laboratory services.

2024

⭐ 6 Clinics & Growing

Today, SAI CLINIC operates 6 successful clinics across different locations, serving thousands of patients with quality healthcare.
