Compassionate Care for Every Family

For over 15 years, SAI CLINIC has been dedicated to providing exceptional healthcare services to the Kavaraipettai community. Our commitment goes beyond medical treatment—we believe in building lasting relationships with our patients and their families.

SAI CLINIC is a multi-specialty healthcare center offering comprehensive medical services including Lab, X-Ray, and ECG. Our team of experienced doctors and healthcare professionals is dedicated to providing personalized care with compassion and expertise.

We are conveniently located at No. 253A, Railway Station Road, Kavaraipettai, Chennai - 601206, making quality healthcare accessible to the entire community.
